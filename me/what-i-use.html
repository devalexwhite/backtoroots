I always have fun reading what tech and gear others use, so here's mine (up-to-date as of the "posted" date).

Tech

This section changes the most, I'm a habitual technology switcher!

  • MacBook Pro M1
  • iPhone 17 Pro Max
  • Apple Watch Ultra 3
  • AirPods Pro (gen 3? 2? no idea)
  • iPad Pro M5

    It's a bit funny my iPad is more powerful than my laptop, but it makes for a great photo editing device.

  • Sony Alpha a7 IV
  • Nintendo Switch 2

Cycling

Go outside an ride a bike, it's good for you!

  • Specialized S-Works Aethos w/Shimano Ultegra di2 groupset and Roval wheels

    My pride and joy, purchased in China during our last trip. To say this bike is fast would be an under statement!

  • Vaast A/1

    My gravel bike and commuter.

  • RadRover 4

    My wife and I bike a pair of these e-bikes to pull the kids around town. Perfect for going to the park or dinner without arriving sweaty from pulling 2 kids in a trailer.

Software

Another category that changes a lot!

  • Nova Editor by Panic

    I love the software Panic used to make (before they became a games company), and Nova is a fantastic example. It's a joy to use and makes me want to code.

  • Alfred 5

    Alfred is Spotlight but good, Raycast but unbloated. I've had a license for a very long time and always renew when a major version ships!

  • Acorn

    A seriously awesome, fast and native image editor for Mac OS.

  • iTerm2

    There may be many terminal options on Mac OS, but like...why? iTerm 2 is the best, and it's free!

  • Things 3

    Another beautiful Mac OS application, I have licenses for Things on Mac, iPad and iPhone. It's a joy to use!

  • Bear

    I love how Bear looks and the UX of it. I have a subscription, but I'll be honest, I'm still trying to get myself to properly use it. I don't take notes a lot beyond random scribbles, but I feel like Bear deserves more...
