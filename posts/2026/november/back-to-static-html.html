Back

It's that time of day again where I rebuild this site! But hold on, there's a few reasons this time, not just me having fun!

The Age of Exploits

It's no secret that running a website has become a bit dangerous lately. For example, the critical PHP exploits that recently led to public warnings from the NSA, FBI and even Japanese Prime Minister. This specific incident impacted my old website, as it ran on PHP hosted by a VPS (virtual private server). I had to login to my server, upgrade PHP and reboot. Not a big deal, but I honest forgot to do it.

With the expectation that AI will further increase the rate at which exploits are found, I decided to move my site to static hosting. This vastly reduces that possibility of it being exploited, while also reducing the need for me to pay for and maintain a server.

Being Lazy

My first step towards migrating this site was...not good. In an interest of not investing too much time, I prompted Codex to build and migrate my site as a Hugo statically built website. It created a theme, migrated my articles and deployed to Vercel. I wasn't proud of it, in fact I felt completely disconnected from it. It wasn't mine and it had no personality, despite the articles being 100% my own words. In my mind, a personal website is the artistic canvas that presents your writing, and I had automated that away.

This morning I ran across this video. Hayyoth demonstrated the difference between AI generated websites and those crafted with a purpose, a soul. He explained the importance of handcrafting your site.

So here we are!

The Great Rebuild

Static, raw HTML handwritten in the Nova text editor, hosted on Cloudflare. That's the present and future of this site. I want this site to be a journal. Not only in the sense of the writing it holds, but also in the evolution of it's sitemap, CSS and content. Something in 5 years I can flip through the pages of it's history to see the evolution of both the site and myself.

All that to say, I'm starting at page 1. It's mostly blank, but I'll continue filling it out as I go. I've named this project "Back to Roots", the goal being the rediscovery of why I got into coding and writing. To that end, it goes without saying, but all content, code and images posted on this site will be made with LLM assistance. This is a playground for myself, and only myself.
